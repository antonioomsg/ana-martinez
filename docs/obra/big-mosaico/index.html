BIG MOSAICO

Big mosaic

octubre, 2012

ES

Big mosaico es un taller donde reinventamos el histórico arte del mosaico romano llevándolo al lenguaje actual. Un grupo de niñ@s realizaron una composición sobre el suelo del patio de las columnas del Palacio de Viana, en Córdoba. Utilizando como teselas, pelotas de tenis recicladas, previamente pintadas con cal de colores. El resultado fue un gran mosaico de formas ondulantes que se superpone al pavimento del patio.

EN

Big mosaico is the name for a children workshop in which we reinvented the traditional mosaic art technic and give to it a contemporary language. A group of some more of 40 children made a mosaic on the ground using recycled painted tennis balls as tesserae. This workshop was hold in a traditional andalusian patio at Viana Palace, in Córdoba. The result was an amazing huge colorful mosaic that gave the patio good and new looking air of modernity. The children had a great time and worked hard with confidence. They were very proud of their work of atr which stayed for 15 days, so that the visitors could see it.
