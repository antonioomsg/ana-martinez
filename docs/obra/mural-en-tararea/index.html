MURAL EN TARAREA

Wall mural painting at Tararea School of Music

octubre, 2014

ES

Tararea es el nombre de una escuela de música muy especial. En Tararea se enseña música desde 0 a 100 años, y lo hacen con un método original en que que prima la experiencia sobre el estudio y aquello de aprender tarareando. En un lugar tan especial, tuve la suerte de realizar una pintura mural que reflejaba el fluir de sonidos musicales de manera visual. La pared, larguísima, me permitió dibujar una larga composición jugando con las lineas de color que se enredaban entre si como las ondas en continuo y fluido movimiento espacial.

EN

Tararea’ is the name for a very special music school in Spain. They teach people from 0 to 100 years old. They use an original method of learning music based on learning from experimenting and playing with instruments rather than studing . In such a special place I wanted to produce a mural painting that represented music in an abstrac way. And so while hearing some of the music they were performing, I got the inspiration to paint it like if it were different waves that flew on the air. The wall was a huge long one, and it allowed me to painte all the way through in a continuous movement of coloful lines.
